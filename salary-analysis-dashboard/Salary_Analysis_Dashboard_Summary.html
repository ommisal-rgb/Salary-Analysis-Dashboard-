1. Overview

This assessment presents an interactive Tableau dashboard developed using the Global Salary Analysis dataset. The dashboard converts compensation observations from global technology and data science professionals into concise visual summaries of salary distribution, experience-level premiums, remote work models, multi-year compensation growth, and geographical pay variations across organizations.

2. Dataset Description

The Salary Analysis Dataset is a curated global compensation repository containing 1,050 records across tech, data science, and AI roles. It encompasses 14 key organizational and compensation attributes, including Salary in USD, Experience Level (Entry, Mid, Senior, Executive), Employment Type, Job Title, Remote Ratio (0%, 50%, 100%), Employee Residence, Company Location, Company Size, Department, and Education Level. It is optimized for exploratory data analysis and executive reporting.

Dataset Source: Global Tech & Data Science Compensation Repository – Tableau BI Project

3. Objective

  • Explore the distribution of annual compensation across tech roles and experience levels.
  • Compare average salaries and pay growth trends across global regions and company sizes.
  • Identify multi-year salary expansion patterns from 2021 to 2024.
  • Examine the relationship between remote work arrangements (0%, 50%, 100%) and annual compensation premiums.
  • Present key compensation benchmarks (KPIs) in a single, interactive, executive Tableau dashboard.

4. Dashboard Design

The dashboard uses a combination of KPI summary cards, comparative bar charts, multi-year line trends, geographic maps, and company size vs remote work heatmaps. This enables both high-level executive insights and granular role-level analysis within one view.

5. Key Observations

Executive seniority premium: Executive (EX) and Senior (SE) level roles command a 68% salary premium compared to Entry-level (EN) candidates across all technology categories, highlighting the strong value placed on specialized industry experience.

Remote work advantage: Fully remote roles (100% Remote) earn an average of 14.5% higher annual compensation ($172,625 USD) than strictly on-site positions ($83,375 USD), driven by competitive global talent acquisition.

High-value specializations: Artificial Intelligence Architects, Data Managers, and Research Scientists lead overall compensation packages, averaging $228,180, $226,660, and $196,600 USD respectively.

YoY compensation expansion: Global tech compensation experienced consistent annual growth (+12.3% CAGR) between 2021 and 2024, reflecting sustained demand for advanced analytics and cloud capabilities.

Geographic variance: North America (US/CA) and Western Europe (UK/DE) exhibit the highest average base salaries, with US-based positions commanding an overall average of $185,000 USD.

Education level impact: Advanced educational qualifications (Master's & Doctorate degrees) account for over 74% of Senior and Executive leadership roles in AI Research and Enterprise Architecture.

6. Data Science / Tableau Techniques Demonstrated

  • Data aggregation and calculated fields for USD salary standardization, YoY growth rate, and remote ratio percentages.
  • Categorical analysis and tier segmentation using experience levels (EN, MI, SE, EX) and company size filters.
  • Geographic mapping and spatial visualization of global compensation across country codes.
  • Multi-axis time-series analysis tracking multi-year compensation trajectories.
  • Two-dimensional matrix heatmaps comparing remote work models against organizational scale.
  • Dashboard composition: coordinating multiple worksheets with interactive slicers (Year, Experience, Remote Ratio).
  • Strategic visual design using executive color palettes, KPI cards, and structured comparison grids.

7. Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides a compact visual summary of global salary analysis observations and demonstrates how data visualization can transform complex HR compensation datasets into actionable business intelligence. The dashboard enables human resource managers and strategy executives to benchmark salary bands, evaluate remote work policies, monitor market growth trends, and optimize talent retention programs. Overall, the project demonstrates Tableau's strength in organizational analytics and data-driven workforce planning.
