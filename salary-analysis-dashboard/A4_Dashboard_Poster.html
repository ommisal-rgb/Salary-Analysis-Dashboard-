💡 Key Analytical Insights

  • Executive Seniority Premium: Executive (EX) and Senior (SE) level roles command a 68% salary premium compared to Entry-level (EN) candidates across all tech categories.
  • Remote Work Advantage: Fully remote roles (100% Remote) earn an average of 14.5% higher annual compensation than strictly on-site positions, driven by global talent recruitment.
  • High-Value Specializations: Artificial Intelligence Architects and Research Scientists lead average compensation packages at $248,000 and $205,000 USD respectively.
  • YoY Compensation Expansion: Overall data science salaries experienced a healthy Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of +12.3% from 2021 to 2024.
  • Geographic Variance: North America (US/CA) and Western Europe (UK/DE) exhibit the highest average base salaries, with US roles averaging $185,000 USD.
  • Education Impact: Advanced degrees (Master's & Doctorate) account for over 74% of Senior and Leadership roles in AI Research and Data Architecture.
